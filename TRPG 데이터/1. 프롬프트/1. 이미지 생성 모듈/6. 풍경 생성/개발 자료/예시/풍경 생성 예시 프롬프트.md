# 4. 풍경 일러스트 예시 프롬프트

## 사용 원칙

아래 예시는 구조 참고용이다. 실제 요청에서는 사용자 최신 지시, 설정 정본, 종족 데이터, 참고 이미지를 우선한다.

모든 예시는 독립 풍경 일러스트이며 배틀맵, 탑다운 맵, 캐릭터 포트레이트, 세션 하이라이트 이미지가 아니다.

## 예시 1. 골리앗 고산 부족 풍경

```text
Create one standalone cinematic fantasy landscape illustration in a wide 16:9 composition. The environment itself is the primary subject; this is not a character portrait, not a narrative action scene, not a battlemap, and not a top-down map.

Depict an isolated goliath tribal settlement at the highest mountain peaks, far above the tree line and partly above the clouds, where thin air, frigid winds, exposed rock, permanent snowfields, and violent weather define daily survival. The main landmark is a vast wind-battered gathering hall built from interlocked dark stone slabs, mammoth ribs, heavy timber, hide screens, and thick rope, standing on a narrow saddle between two jagged peaks.

Show the settlement as a harsh but functional nomadic highland culture: low stone shelters embedded into the mountain for protection, hide-covered tents that can be dismantled, smoke rising from recessed fire pits, suspended rope bridges, climbing routes marked by carved stones, trophy cairns recording notable deeds, communal lifting stones, hunting racks, and weathered banners snapping in the gale. Small groups of seven-to-eight-foot-tall goliaths with gray and blue-gray stone-like skin, powerful builds, shaved heads, geometric tattoos, heavy hide and fur clothing, climbing gear, spears, and packs may appear throughout the settlement as distant secondary figures. They must remain small environmental scale cues engaged in hauling supplies, training, hunting preparation, tending fires, and crossing the ridge; no single goliath should dominate the image or pose as a hero.

Set the scene in cold late-afternoon light after a snow squall, with broken sunlight piercing through storm clouds, wind-driven ice crystals crossing the ridge, blue-gray shadows, pale limestone snow, dark volcanic rock, and restrained warm orange firelight inside the shelters. Use a slightly elevated panoramic camera that reveals the settlement, the dangerous drop-offs, and the endless chain of frozen peaks beyond, with clear foreground rock textures, a readable midground settlement, and immense atmospheric depth in the background.

Render it as high-end cinematic fantasy landscape illustration, grounded fantasy realism, AAA game environment quality, realistic stone, fur, hide, rope, snow, ice, timber, smoke, and physically believable mountain light. Keep the design detailed but compositionally clean and visually readable. Do not include a central hero, close-up faces, dramatic combat, spell effects, modern mountaineering equipment, text, captions, labels, logos, UI, borders, watermarks, split panels, character-sheet layouts, visible grids, tactical-map framing, isometric-map framing, or a true top-down camera.
```

## 예시 2. 드워프 지하 연맹 수도

```text
Create one standalone cinematic fantasy landscape illustration in a wide 16:9 composition. The environment itself is the primary subject; this is not a character portrait, not a battlemap, and not a top-down map.

Depict a colossal dwarven federation capital carved through the interior of a volcanic mountain. The main visual landmark is a monumental stepped citadel cut directly into a black basalt wall, connected to the surrounding city by massive stone bridges spanning a glowing chasm.

Build the city from layered terraces, vaulted halls, forge districts, ore lifts, rail-like cargo tracks, water channels, carved ancestor pillars, heavy iron gates, suspended cranes, and thousands of warm windows embedded in the rock. Show small dwarven workers, merchants, guards, and engineers as secondary environmental figures distributed across bridges, lifts, markets, and forge balconies; they must communicate scale and daily life without becoming individual portrait subjects.

Use a slightly elevated panoramic camera from a foreground balcony, with dark stone and brass in the foreground, the citadel and forge districts in the midground, and vast cavern walls disappearing into smoke and reflected firelight in the background. Use realistic basalt, granite, iron, brass, timber, soot, steam, molten metal, and warm furnace light against cool subterranean shadows.

Render it as a high-end cinematic fantasy landscape illustration with grounded realism and AAA game environment quality. Keep the city readable, coherent, and inhabited without turning it into an isometric map or tactical layout. Do not include a central hero, combat, visible grids, labels, text, UI, logos, borders, watermarks, or a true top-down camera.
```

## 예시 3. 구름 위 드래곤 대륙

```text
Create one standalone cinematic high-fantasy landscape illustration in a wide 21:9 composition. The environment itself is the primary subject.

Depict an immense verdant continent suspended above an endless sea of clouds, the ancestral homeland of dragons. The main landmark is a chain of colossal mountain arches rising from the floating landmass, with waterfalls pouring from the cliffs and dissolving into mist far below.

Cover the continent with ancient forests, windswept alpine meadows, crystalline rivers, exposed golden stone, and distant draconic sanctuaries integrated into the mountain ridges. A few dragons may appear as tiny silhouettes gliding between peaks to establish scale, but no dragon should dominate the frame or become a creature portrait.

Use luminous morning light above the cloud layer, deep blue sky, bright white clouds, saturated but natural greens, warm gold stone, subtle atmospheric haze, and immense depth. Frame the scene from a high cliff in the foreground, looking across the continent toward distant peaks and storm systems.

Render it as high-end cinematic fantasy landscape art with grounded environmental realism, realistic vegetation, water, stone, clouds, and physically believable sunlight. Do not include a central character, close-up dragon, battle, text, labels, UI, borders, watermarks, visible grids, tactical-map framing, or a true top-down camera.
```

## 예시 4. 고대 마법 도시의 황혼

```text
Create one standalone cinematic fantasy cityscape illustration in a wide 16:9 composition. The city itself is the primary subject.

Depict an ancient arcane city built around a circular lake at twilight. The main visual landmark is a broad, blunt-topped central mage tower rising from an island of white stone, connected to eight surrounding districts by luminous bridges and low monumental causeways.

Show layered neighborhoods of observatories, libraries, workshops, gardens, markets, water stairs, and public plazas. Arcane technology should be integrated into architecture through restrained glowing channels, levitating navigation lights, and slow-moving transport platforms rather than excessive magical particles. Small citizens, boats, carts, and bridge traffic should make the city feel inhabited without becoming individual subjects.

Use a distant elevated city overview, with foreground rooftops, the central lake and tower in the midground, and surrounding hills under a violet-blue evening sky. Combine cool twilight, warm window light, pale stone, oxidized copper roofs, dark water reflections, and restrained cyan magical accents.

Render it as high-end cinematic fantasy cityscape illustration, grounded fantasy realism, AAA environment quality, with detailed but clean architecture and coherent perspective. Do not include a central hero, combat, giant spell effects, text, labels, logos, UI, borders, watermarks, visible grids, isometric-map framing, or a true top-down camera.
```

## 예시 5. 다크 판타지 순례 성소

```text
Create one standalone cinematic dark-fantasy landscape illustration in a wide 16:9 composition. The environment itself is the primary subject; this is not a character scene and not a battlemap.

Depict an ancient pilgrimage sanctuary built into a barren mountain valley under a heavy overcast sky. The main landmark is a colossal weathered stone gate shaped like two kneeling giants, opening toward a distant black monastery cut into the cliff.

A long processional road crosses ash-covered ground, ruined prayer pillars, dry channels, and abandoned shelters. Small groups of cloaked pilgrims and pack animals may appear as distant secondary figures moving toward the monastery, used only to communicate scale and hardship. No individual face or heroic pose should be visible.

Use cold gray daylight, muted brown stone, black iron, patches of old snow, restrained amber lanterns, and low drifting cloud that does not obscure the sanctuary. Compose the scene from a low foreground position beside the road, using the giant gate to frame the monastery and the valley beyond.

Render it as high-end cinematic dark-fantasy landscape illustration with grounded realism, realistic stone, ash, cloth, iron, snow, and atmospheric depth. Keep the location ominous but readable. Do not include a central character, combat, monsters, excessive gore, text, labels, UI, borders, watermarks, visible grids, tactical-map framing, or a true top-down camera.
```
