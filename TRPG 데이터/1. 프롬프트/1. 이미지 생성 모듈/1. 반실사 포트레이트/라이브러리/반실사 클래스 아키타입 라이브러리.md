# 4. 반실사 클래스 아키타입 라이브러리

## Purpose

A class or archetype is a visual behavior system.

It controls:

- combat range
- body pressure
- movement language
- weapon identity
- armor or outfit direction
- posture
- silhouette
- power source only when specified

It does not automatically decide morality, religion, culture, or magical glow.

## 스타일 상속

이 라이브러리는 `../../라이브러리/스타일/붓터치 반실사.md`를 스타일 정본으로 참조한다. 이 문서는 대상·문화권·프리셋·클래스·테마 데이터만 제공하며 독립적인 렌더링 화풍을 강제하지 않는다.

## Class Literalism Rule

Interpret class words literally.

Do not convert:

- Paladin into "holy warrior" unless holy power, oath, deity, or sacred theme is specified
- Cleric into radiant priest unless a divine visual source is specified
- Warlock into infernal villain unless a patron or infernal theme is specified
- Ranger into glowing nature spirit unless magical nature power is specified
- Rogue into assassin unless stealth-killer identity is specified
- Monk into wuxia / East Asian martial artist unless the cultural anchor supports it
- Barbarian into primitive savage unless the user's concept supports it
- Bard into comedic performer unless tone supports it

A class is first a silhouette, equipment, and behavior identity.

## Rank Interaction

Normal:

- grounded outfit
- practical gear
- restrained pose
- minimal or no FX

Elite:

- clearer signature equipment
- more confident pose
- controlled FX if justified
- stronger silhouette

Legendary:

- iconic silhouette
- dramatic scale
- powerful but readable FX
- environmental storytelling

Apprentice / novice / low-level:

- no excessive aura
- simpler equipment
- imperfect or modest gear
- grounded expression and posture

## Core Combat Range

### Melee

Use:

- grounded stance
- visible melee weapon
- forward pressure
- readable grip
- armor or clothing suited to close combat

### Ranged

Use:

- bow, crossbow, thrown weapon, sling, javelin, or magical ranged focus
- aiming or ready posture
- clear line of sight
- lean silhouette or stable marksman stance

### Caster

Use:

- staff, wand, tome, orb, charm, ritual tool, or hand gestures
- controlled magical light
- robe or outfit construction appropriate to tradition
- readable face and hands

### Hybrid

Use:

- one dominant weapon and one secondary power source
- balanced pose
- readable priority
- avoid too many visual systems

### Summoner

Use:

- commanding hand gesture
- focus object
- subtle summoning circle or restrained spirit indication

Avoid:

- adding a full second character unless explicitly requested
- background avatar dominating the subject

## Core Combat Weight

Light:

- agile body language
- lightweight armor
- cloth, leather, flexible gear
- narrow silhouette

Medium:

- practical combat gear
- balanced armor
- adaptable stance
- moderate silhouette weight

Heavy:

- thick armor
- broad shoulders
- grounded stance
- defensive or frontline pressure

Massive:

- overwhelming scale
- large body mass
- heavy weapons
- slow but dominant posture

Fragile:

- thin body
- delicate posture
- magical or intellectual identity
- avoid heavy armor unless intentionally ironic

## Core Combat Style

Defensive:

- shield
- guarded stance
- stable posture
- protective silhouette

Aggressive:

- forward lean
- raised weapon
- explosive action
- intense gaze

Precision:

- controlled pose
- narrow weapon alignment
- refined technique
- sharp silhouette

Stealth:

- low-key lighting
- compact silhouette
- hood, cloak, soft gear if appropriate
- predatory stillness
- do not automatically add shadow magic

Brutal:

- heavy weapon
- scars
- rough materials
- violent posture

Tactical:

- practical equipment
- measured stance
- visible tools
- calm expression

Mobile:

- dynamic cloth
- active posture
- off-center movement
- light equipment

## Power Source Handling

Power source must be user-specified or strongly implied.

Available power sources:

- Physical
- Arcane
- Divine
- Nature
- Shadow
- Void
- Blood
- Necrotic
- Elemental
- Spiritual
- Mechanical
- Psionic
- Alchemical
- None / mundane

If none is specified, use grounded physical presentation.

## Core Classes

### Fighter

Visual identity:

- Weapon specialist, practical combatant.

Use:

- clear primary weapon
- practical armor
- strong stance
- combat-worn gear

Do not add:

- divine aura
- arcane effects
- noble status unless specified

### Knight

Visual identity:

- Armored melee combatant with structured silhouette.

Use:

- plate, mail, shield, sword, lance, mace, or polearm
- heavy or medium armor
- disciplined posture

Do not automatically add:

- holy light
- royal symbols
- cathedral background

### Paladin

Visual identity:

- Oath-bound armored warrior.

Use:

- armor
- symbolic weapon or shield
- disciplined presence
- oath, order, deity, or code only if specified

If holy paladin is specified:

- use sacred light, gold-white accents, sanctified weapon

If dark, fallen, oathless, low-rank, or mundane paladin is specified:

- do not use holy glow
- show armor, oath-symbol, scars, worn equipment, restrained presence

### Cleric

Visual identity:

- Faith-associated support, ritual, healer, battle priest, scholar, or shrine keeper.

Use:

- holy symbol only if specified
- ritual focus
- mace, shield, staff, book, incense, relic, or medicine kit depending on concept

Do not automatically add:

- radiant aura
- church background
- plate armor
- angelic beauty

### Ranger

Visual identity:

- Tracker, hunter, scout, wilderness combatant.

Use:

- bow, spear, twin blades, traps, cloak, travel gear
- leather, cloth, practical packs
- outdoor posture

Do not automatically add:

- green aura
- spirit animal
- glowing leaves
- druidic magic

### Rogue

Visual identity:

- Skill, agility, opportunistic movement, tools.

Use:

- daggers, shortsword, crossbow, lockpicks, cloak, light armor
- compact pose
- sharp gaze

Do not automatically add:

- assassin murder tone
- shadow magic
- black-only outfit

### Assassin

Visual identity:

- Stealth killer, targeted violence, predatory posture.

Use:

- daggers, curved blades, garrote, poisoned blade, compact weapons
- low-key lighting
- precise posture

### Barbarian / Berserker

Visual identity:

- Raw physical force, survival pressure, uncontrolled or ritualized violence.

Use:

- large weapon
- scars
- fur, leather, rough materials
- powerful body
- aggressive posture if berserker

Do not automatically make:

- primitive stereotype
- half-naked body
- blood-covered rage unless requested

### Wizard

Visual identity:

- Study, spell structure, books, arcane control.

Use:

- robe, staff, tome, wand, ritual geometry
- intelligent posture
- controlled magic

### Sorcerer

Visual identity:

- Innate magic, body-born power, expressive energy.

Use:

- internal glow if specified
- expressive hands
- magical atmosphere tied to bloodline or origin

### Warlock

Visual identity:

- Pact magic, forbidden knowledge, patron-touched presence.

Use:

- pact mark, occult focus, dark tome, strange jewelry, patron motif if specified

Do not automatically add:

- horns
- demons
- infernal red glow
- evil expression

### Druid

Visual identity:

- Nature ritualist, shapeshifter, wilderness priest, primal caster.

Use:

- natural materials
- staff, sickle, antlers, leaves, animal motifs if specified
- organic design

Do not automatically add:

- high-intensity glowing green aura
- full animal companion unless requested

### Monk

Visual identity:

- Disciplined body, unarmed technique, controlled movement.

Use:

- simple garments
- wraps
- balanced stance
- body-focused silhouette

Do not automatically assign:

- East Asian setting
- wuxia robes
- qi effects

unless requested.

### Bard

Visual identity:

- Performance, story, charm, travel, court, song, magic through art.

Use:

- instrument, rapier, flamboyant but controlled outfit, travel clothes, stage or court motifs

Do not automatically make:

- comic relief
- modern musician
- exaggerated clown design

### Artificer / Alchemist

Visual identity:

- Fantasy inventor, tool user, magical engineer, potion craft, chemical magic.

Use:

- tools, arcane devices, leather apron, brass details, runic machinery
- vials, satchel, stained gloves, burner, alchemical symbols

Avoid:

- modern science fiction unless requested
- cyberpunk unless requested

## Monster Combat Archetypes

### Predator

Use:

- forward head
- claws or teeth
- low stance
- hunting posture

### Guardian

Use:

- broad stance
- heavy body
- protective posture
- stone, armor, shield, or ritual boundary motifs

### Brute

Use:

- huge body mass
- heavy limbs
- impact posture
- simple but powerful silhouette

### Controller

Use:

- central gaze or core
- commanding posture
- magical or psychic visual focus
- clear focal anatomy

### Skirmisher

Use:

- agile stance
- light limbs
- motion-ready posture
- sharp silhouette
