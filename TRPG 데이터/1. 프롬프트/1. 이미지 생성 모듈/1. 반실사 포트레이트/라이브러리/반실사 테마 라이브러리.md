# 3. 반실사 테마 라이브러리

## Purpose

A theme is a visual layer, not a complete character.

Themes control:

- color palette
- lighting behavior
- FX density
- material language
- atmosphere
- environmental support
- emotional tone

When generating directly, convert themes into natural production instructions.
Do not output bare keyword tags.

## 스타일 상속

이 라이브러리는 `../../라이브러리/스타일/붓터치 반실사.md`를 스타일 정본으로 참조한다. 이 문서는 대상·문화권·프리셋·클래스·테마 데이터만 제공하며 독립적인 렌더링 화풍을 강제하지 않는다.

## Unified Theme Motif System

A theme is not just an aura, color filter, or background effect.

When a theme is selected, integrate it through:

- body surface
- outfit or armor
- weapon or equipment
- ornaments
- FX
- environment

The theme should feel designed into the character, not pasted on after the character is finished.

Direct motif integration phrase:

```text
Integrate the selected theme across the subject's anatomy, clothing or armor, weapon, ornaments, magical effects, lighting, and surrounding environment so the image reads as one coherent fantasy archetype.
```

## Theme Combination Rule

A subject may combine multiple themes.

Examples:

- Frost + Arcane = cold magical energy, crystalline glow, blue-violet spell light
- Shadow + Noble = dark refined elegance, controlled low-key lighting
- Nature + Ancient = weathered organic materials, moss, bark, old sacred forest mood
- Blood + Savage = crimson motion, violent pressure, battle-worn surfaces
- Void + Eldritch = cosmic darkness, spatial distortion, alien unease

Use only the FX and atmosphere justified by the concept and rank.

## Fire

Core identity:

- Destructive heat, flame, ash, wrath, forge, volcanic power.

Colors:

- ember orange
- crimson
- deep red
- blackened ash
- molten yellow accents

FX:

- controlled flames
- ember sparks
- heat shimmer
- smoke trails
- molten cracks

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use controlled ember light, scorched materials, and volcanic warmth without letting flames obscure the face, weapon, or silhouette.
```

## Frost

Core identity:

- Cold power, ice, silence, winter, preservation, crystalline elegance.

Colors:

- pale blue
- icy white
- cold cyan
- silver
- frozen crystal blue

FX:

- frost mist
- snow particles
- ice shards
- frozen breath
- thin crystalline aura

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use cold blue-white lighting, frost mist, and crystalline material accents while keeping the subject sharply readable.
```

## Lightning

Core identity:

- Speed, impact, storm, nerve-like energy, sudden violence.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use restrained lightning arcs and storm-lit contrast, with the energy supporting the pose rather than covering the body.
```

## Nature

Core identity:

- Life, wilderness, growth, spirit, wood, leaves, primal green energy.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use grounded wilderness materials, dappled forest light, and subtle organic details without turning every ranger or druid into a glowing magical figure unless requested.
```

## Shadow

Core identity:

- Darkness, stealth, secrecy, fear, corrupted movement, hidden intent.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use low-key shadow atmosphere and dark materials while preserving the face, eyes, weapon, and outer silhouette.
```

## Holy / Sacred

Core identity:

- Sacred power, divinity, oath, temple light, sanctified presence.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use sacred gold-white light and refined noble materials only when the subject is explicitly divine, sacred, or holy. Do not apply this automatically to every paladin or cleric.
```

## Arcane

Core identity:

- Magic, study, ancient knowledge, spellcraft, geometry, energy control.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use restrained arcane geometry, blue-violet magical light, and scholarly or mystical materials while keeping the face and silhouette clear.
```

## Poison

Core identity:

- Toxicity, venom, alchemy, corruption, sickness, dangerous beauty.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use poison-green atmosphere and toxic material accents without covering the head or weapon with thick vapor.
```

## Blood

Core identity:

- Life force, violence, sacrifice, rage, wounds, ritual, crimson magic.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use crimson pressure, battle-worn material details, and controlled blood-themed FX without making the composition unreadable or gratuitously messy.
```

## Void

Core identity:

- Cosmic absence, gravity, nothingness, reality distortion, alien emptiness.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use cosmic darkness and subtle spatial distortion while preserving anatomy, silhouette, and material readability.
```

## Necrotic

Core identity:

- Death, decay, undeath, grave magic, rot, old curses.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use decayed material language and grave-colored atmosphere without turning the subject into a generic skeleton unless requested.
```

## Celestial

Core identity:

- Stars, heavens, astral order, divine cosmos, serene transcendence.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use serene astral lighting and refined celestial materials while avoiding excessive bloom or unreadable glowing forms.
```

## Abyssal / Infernal

Core identity:

- Hellish pressure, demonic force, abyssal heat, chains, sulfur, danger.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use dark iron, ember glow, smoke, and controlled infernal pressure only when the concept explicitly requires abyssal or infernal identity.
```

## Mechanical

Core identity:

- Artificial body, engineered violence, arcane machine logic, metal mass.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use worn metal plating, bolts, seams, pistons, soot, oil, scratches, and a readable mechanical head focal point without adding human skin.
```

## Noble / Royal

Core identity:

- Authority, lineage, ceremony, composure, refined materials.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use refined posture, controlled ornamentation, high-quality fabric or armor, and symbolic accessories without turning the subject into a generic prince or princess.
```

## Ancient / Ruined

Core identity:

- Age, memory, weathering, lost civilization, old magic.

Direct instruction phrase:

```text
Use weathered materials, cracked surfaces, faded ornaments, dust, moss, and ancient atmosphere while keeping the character design readable.
```
