# 6. 반실사 캐릭터 프리셋 라이브러리

## Purpose

A preset is a reusable visual direction package.

It is not a finished character.
It should be adapted to the user's subject, rank, species, class, culture, and mood.

When generating directly, use preset language as natural image instructions.

## 스타일 상속

이 라이브러리는 `../../라이브러리/스타일/붓터치 반실사.md`를 스타일 정본으로 참조한다. 이 문서는 대상·문화권·프리셋·클래스·테마 데이터만 제공하며 독립적인 렌더링 화풍을 강제하지 않는다.

## Preset Intensity Rule

Normal preset:

- grounded details
- restrained FX
- simple background
- practical silhouette

Elite preset:

- stronger visual identity
- controlled FX
- more distinctive equipment
- cinematic lighting

Legendary preset:

- iconic silhouette
- environmental scale
- powerful but readable FX
- symbolic atmosphere
- vertical premium high-end fantasy illustration cinematic 3D character-render presentation when appropriate
- unified motif integration across subject, equipment, FX, and environment

## Preset: Grounded Adventurer

Identity:

- A practical TRPG adventurer with believable gear and restrained fantasy styling.

Use:

- worn travel clothes
- leather, cloth, mail, or simple plate depending on class
- backpack, belt pouches, bedroll, rope, lantern, small tools
- modest weapon
- grounded expression

Direct phrase:

```text
Create a grounded realistic semi-realistic painterly fantasy adventurer portrait with practical travel gear, worn materials, clear class equipment, and minimal supernatural FX.
```
