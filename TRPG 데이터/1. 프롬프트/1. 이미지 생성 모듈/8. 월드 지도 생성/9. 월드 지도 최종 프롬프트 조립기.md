# 9. 월드 지도 최종 프롬프트 조립기

## 목적

이 문서는 네 가지 지도 생성 모드의 최종 영어 프롬프트를 조립한다.

## 조립 순서

1. 지도 종류
2. 랜덤 또는 사용자 지정 모드
3. 사용자 제공 구조
4. 보존 규칙
5. 지형과 랜드마크
6. 공통 양피지 양식
7. 200픽셀 바다 여백
8. 렌더링 스타일
9. 제외 요소

## 공통 최종 프롬프트 템플릿

```text
Create one high-quality 16:9 colorful fantasy {MAP_SCOPE} painted on a large sheet of aged parchment.

Map mode: {MAP_MODE}

Geographic design:
{GEOGRAPHIC_DESIGN}

Terrain and regions:
{TERRAIN_AND_REGIONS}

Major landmarks:
{MAJOR_LANDMARKS}

Reference preservation:
{REFERENCE_PRESERVATION}

Preserve the internal terrain layout, biome positions, major landmarks, and geographic relationships as faithfully as possible, while adapting the coastline and outer landmass silhouette into a more natural, believable, and cartographically coherent form.

The parchment must have visible old paper fibers, subtle stains, discoloration, and a handcrafted illustrated-map texture. Its outer edge must be torn, rough, irregular, and weathered. Everything outside the parchment must be a solid matte black background.

Fill the inner map area all the way toward the parchment boundary with rich blue ocean water, leaving only a clearly visible narrow band of torn brown parchment around the outside. Do not leave a broad empty beige parchment margin inside the map. Do not allow the blue ocean to extend outside the torn paper.

Keep every major landmass away from the parchment boundary. Preserve approximately 200 pixels of blue ocean margin on the top, bottom, left, and right sides between the nearest coastline and the inner parchment edge. When exact pixels cannot be measured, interpret this as roughly ten to twelve percent of the image dimensions on every side.

Render the terrain as colorful, polished high-fantasy cartography. Keep coastlines, mountain chains, rivers, forests, deserts, snowfields, swamps, volcanic zones, crystal lands, plains, cliffs, and biome boundaries clearly readable. Use painterly detail and restrained topographic depth while keeping the image useful as a geographic reference rather than a cinematic landscape scene.

Do not add text, labels, banners, compass roses, scale bars, grids, political borders, decorative emblems, interface elements, or watermarks unless explicitly requested. Do not let any landmass touch the parchment edge. Do not place black background inside the parchment. Do not replace the required blue ocean margin with empty beige paper.
```

## 변수

- `{MAP_SCOPE}`: world map / continent map
- `{MAP_MODE}`: randomly generated / based on the provided references
- `{GEOGRAPHIC_DESIGN}`: 대륙 수, 위치, 외곽선, 해양 구조
- `{TERRAIN_AND_REGIONS}`: 지형과 생태권
- `{MAJOR_LANDMARKS}`: 핵심 랜드마크
- `{REFERENCE_PRESERVATION}`: 사용자 이미지 보존 지시

## 랜덤 모드 처리

랜덤 모드에서는 보존 문장을 다음으로 바꾼다.

```text
Design an original and geographically coherent arrangement. Use natural coastlines, believable mountain chains, connected drainage systems, varied climate zones, and enough open ocean or coastal space for clear map readability.
```

## 최종 검사

- 양피지 바깥이 검은색인가
- 종이 가장자리가 찢어진 형태인가
- 종이 내부 가장자리가 파란 바다인가
- 상하좌우 약 200픽셀의 바다 여백이 있는가
- 육지가 종이 가장자리에 닿지 않는가
- 지형과 권역이 읽히는가
- 요청하지 않은 텍스트가 없는가
