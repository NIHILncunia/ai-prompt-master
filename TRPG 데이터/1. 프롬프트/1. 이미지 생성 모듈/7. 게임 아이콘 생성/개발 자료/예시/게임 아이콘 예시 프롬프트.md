# 4. 게임 아이콘 예시 프롬프트

## 예시 사용 원칙

아래 영어 프롬프트는 구조 참고용이다. 사용자 요청의 고유명사, 재질, 색상, 속성, 위계를 우선 반영한다.

## 1. 치유 물약 아이콘

```text
Create a high-end fantasy RPG inventory icon as a 1:1 square image. Show one elegant glass healing potion bottle filled with luminous crimson liquid, sealed with a dark cork and a simple leather tie. Use a clear compact silhouette, a slight three-quarter angle, realistic glass reflections, and a restrained warm inner glow. The bottle should occupy roughly 78% of the canvas with clean margin on all sides and remain instantly readable at small UI size. Isolate it against an intentionally visible non-transparent abstract background: a softly blurred deep desaturated teal color field with restrained localized cool mist, chosen to separate the crimson glass and warm glow. No icon frame, no border, no badge rim, no circular UI ring, no card bezel, no text, no numbers, no logo, no table, no floor, no room, no scenery, no large cast shadow, and no effects touching the canvas edges.
```

## 2. 흑요석 전투도끼 아이콘

```text
Create a high-end dark fantasy RPG weapon icon as a 1:1 square image. Show one massive obsidian battle axe in a strong diagonal composition, with the entire haft and blade visible. The axe head is carved from near-black volcanic glass with chipped edges, subtle crimson heat veins, and a worn dark-leather grip. Keep the silhouette heavy and unmistakable, occupying about 80% of the canvas with safe margin. Use controlled rim lighting to reveal the black weapon against an intentionally visible non-transparent abstract background: a softly blurred pale low-saturation blue-gray field with restrained localized violet mist, selected for maximum edge separation. No icon frame, no border, no badge rim, no circular UI ring, no card bezel, no character, no hands, no text, no numbers, no scenery, no floor, no large cast shadow, and no sparks or smoke reaching the canvas edges.
```

## 10. 다중 아이템 요청 확인 예시

```text

```

출력 해석:

```text
생성 수량: 4장
비율: 각 이미지 1:1
출력 방식: 화염검 1장, 냉기도끼 1장, 번개창 1장, 독단검 1장
합성 시트: 생성하지 않음
공통 배경 처리: 흐린 저채도 색면과 절제된 국소 안개, 조명 방향·흐림 정도·안개 밀도는 공통으로 유지
개별 배경색: 각 아이템의 주색·명도·발광에 맞춰 대비가 확보되도록 개별 조정하며, 모두 비투명 추상 배경을 사용
공통 프레임: 없음
```
