# 06. 배경 블록

상태: 승격 확정

```text
Fill the entire square with an opaque colored abstract background harmonized with the subject's element, dominant hue, luminance, and glow. Do not use pure black, near-black, or charcoal as the default background for an occupied slot. Use only restrained color fields, soft gradients, localized mist, energy haze, or sparse particles; no scenery, floor, room, landscape, transparent pixels, alpha holes, or unfilled canvas.
```
