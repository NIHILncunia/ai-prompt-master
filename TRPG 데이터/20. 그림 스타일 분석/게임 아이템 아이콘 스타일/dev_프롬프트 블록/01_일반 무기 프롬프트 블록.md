# 01 일반 무기 프롬프트 블록

```text
Treat any weapon without an explicitly stated magical, elemental, cursed, relic, or legendary property as an ordinary non-magical weapon.
Prioritize weapon-category silhouette, construction, proportions, and readable material separation before ornament.
Render the weapon in a stylized high-quality fantasy RPG inventory-icon language: bold readable shapes, decorative fantasy design, crisp graphic-painterly materials, and polished game-asset finish. Do not drift into photorealistic product rendering.
Use a strictly grayscale opaque abstract background. The background may vary in gray value, texture, and luminance, but must not carry visible blue, brown, green, purple, red, or other hue.
Do not automatically add elemental flames, lightning, magical aura, glowing runes, floating crystals, energy vortexes, or excessive bloom.
Use only subtle rim light when needed to separate a dark weapon from the background.
```
