# 02 속성·마법 무기 프롬프트 블록

```text
Activate magical or elemental effects only when the weapon is explicitly described as magical, elemental, cursed, relic-grade, legendary, or otherwise special.
Keep the weapon's actual silhouette, construction, handle, blade, limbs, barrel, shaft, chain, or staff head clearly readable before any effect.
Use a clearly non-grayscale colored abstract background harmonized with the stated effect. Keep the same stylized high-quality fantasy RPG inventory-icon rendering used for ordinary weapons; the difference comes from the colored background and explicitly requested effect, not from a different base art style.
Use localized glow only as support.
Concentrate magical effects near their plausible source such as the blade edge, rune, gem, magical core, muzzle, or staff head.
Do not let aura, particles, flames, lightning, or bloom cover the weapon body or erase its category silhouette.
Do not use colored slot borders as a rarity indicator.
```
